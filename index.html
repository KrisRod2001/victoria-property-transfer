Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a Section 32 statement?

It discloses vendor, title, and property details, including covenants, easements, and recent building works, mandatory before signing a contract.

What is the cooling-off period?

A 3-business-day period allowing buyers to terminate a contract (excluding auctions) with a monetary penalty.
